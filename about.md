  <div class="eyebrow">ABOUT THE PAPER</div>
  <h1>The work behind the work.</h1>

  <p class="lead">The AI Builder Dispatch is a field paper by Manidhar Pati, a solopreneur and builder working from Warangal, India.</p>

  <p>I use this publication to document the questions, systems, experiments, constraints, failures, and decisions behind the things I build.</p>

  <p>The source material is not limited to finished products. It comes from notebooks, repositories, prototypes, architecture notes, experiments, and the questions that existed before the software did.</p>

  <h2>What you will find here</h2>
  <p>Founder stories. AI systems. Local-first infrastructure. Product experiments. Build logs. Field notes. The unfinished thinking that usually disappears before a polished launch.</p>
